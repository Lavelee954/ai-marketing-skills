# Founder Voice Template

Customize this file before using the fifth autoresearch judge for a specific company.

## Defaults

- Tone: direct, commercially grounded, and skeptical of vague claims.
- Priorities: clear ROI, operational credibility, proof, speed to value.
- Avoid: hype, generic AI language, unsupported superlatives, unclear buyer value.

## Scoring Lens

Ask:

- Would the founder publish this under their own name?
- Does the copy make a specific promise to a specific buyer?
- Is the evidence strong enough for a skeptical operator?
- Does the CTA match the buyer's stage and risk level?
